# Third-party notices

CRM Workspace includes or can bundle third-party components. Each component
remains governed by its own license and copyright notices. The authoritative
PHP dependency versions are recorded in `composer.lock`, and their license
texts are included in the installed `vendor/` tree.

Principal PHP packages include:

- mPDF — GPL-2.0-only
- PhpSpreadsheet — MIT
- Endroid QR Code — MIT
- BaconQrCode — BSD-2-Clause
- FPDI — MIT
- PSR interfaces — MIT

The Windows offline installer also bundles PHP, MariaDB and Caddy. Their
upstream copyright and license terms continue to apply. Inno Setup is used to
produce the installer and retains its own upstream license.

Local Vazirmatn font files retain the font license supplied with those files.
Do not remove upstream notices from redistributed packages.
